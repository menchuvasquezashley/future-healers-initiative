Your FHI Journey

Your healthcare journey starts with curiosity. Explore careers, try new things, serve your community, and build your own path.

🌱 How It Works

01

Explore

Discover healthcare careers, meet professionals, and learn about different paths.

02

Participate

Take part in FHI activities, challenges, workshops, and healthcare experiences.

03

Serve

Use what you learn to participate in service projects and help your community.

04

Lead

Take initiative, help others, and become part of building the future of FHI.

⭐ FHI Points

Attend

Show up and participate in regular FHI meetings and activities.

Complete

Finish challenges, workshops, medical mysteries, and other FHI activities.

Serve

Participate in FHI service projects and community activities.

Lead

Help organize activities, support other students, or take on a leadership role.

📝 FHI Checkpoint

After activities, students can check in and reflect on their experience. Your feedback helps us understand what students actually enjoy and learn from.

  • ⭐ How much did you enjoy the activity? (1–5)
  • 🧠 How much did you learn? (1–5)
  • 💡 What is one thing you learned or found interesting?
  • 🔁 Would you want to do something like this again?
