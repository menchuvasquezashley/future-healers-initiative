🕵️ Medical Mystery #001

The Mystery of the Missing Energy

A student has been feeling tired and having trouble concentrating. Here are a few clues:

  • They have been sleeping around 5 hours a night.
  • They sometimes skip breakfast and lunch.
  • They have been stressed about school.

Which question would be most useful to ask next?

A. What is your favorite subject?
B. What does your sleep, food, and daily routine usually look like?
C. What color do you like?
✅ B! Understanding someone's daily routine gives more useful context. This is a fictional learning activity, not a diagnosis.

💊 Medical Myth or Fact

“Antibiotics can treat viral infections like the common cold.”

Myth or Fact?
❌ Myth. Antibiotics work against bacteria, not viruses such as those that cause the common cold.
