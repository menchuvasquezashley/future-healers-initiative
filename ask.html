💡 What can you ask?

You don't have to know exactly what you want to do yet. Start with a question.

  • What is this healthcare career actually like?
  • How did you know this was the career for you?
  • What should I do in high school if I'm interested in healthcare?
  • What is the difference between different healthcare careers?
  • What experiences helped you get where you are?
  • What do you wish you knew when you were my age?
