SURGEON

Could you see yourself in the OR?

Surgeons combine science, precision, problem-solving, teamwork, and hands-on medicine to care for patients through surgical treatment.

🐀 Future Healer Tip You don't have to know your specialty yet. Exploring is part of the process!

🔎 Get to Know the Career

🔎 What They Do

Surgeons are physicians who diagnose and treat injuries, diseases, and other conditions. They may perform operations as part of a patient's treatment.

🏥 Where They Work

Surgeons may work in hospitals, physicians' offices, surgical centers, and other healthcare settings while working with a larger healthcare team.

🎓 The Path

College → Medical School → Surgical Residency → Practice. Some surgeons continue with fellowship training in a subspecialty.

🧠 Skills

Problem-solving, communication, teamwork, precision, leadership, organization, patience, and compassion all matter.

⏰ A Day in the Life

🌅 Morning

Surgeons may see patients, review cases, participate in rounds, and prepare for procedures.

🏥 During the Day

Their work can include procedures, operations, consultations, patient care, and teamwork with other healthcare professionals.

💬 Throughout the Day

Surgeons communicate with patients and families and coordinate care with other members of the healthcare team.

🌙 End of the Day

Documentation, follow-up care, and planning may all be part of the job.
