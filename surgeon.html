🩺 Surgeon

A healthcare career focused on treating certain conditions through surgical procedures.

What do they do?

Surgeons evaluate patients, work with healthcare teams, perform operations, and help patients through recovery.

🏥 Where do they work?

Hospitals, surgical centers, clinics, and other healthcare settings.

👥 Who do they work with?

Surgeons work with nurses, anesthesiologists, physician assistants, technicians, and many other healthcare professionals.

🎓 Education Path

  • High school
  • College / undergraduate degree
  • Medical school
  • Surgical residency
  • Additional training depending on specialty

🧠 Skills

  • Problem solving
  • Attention to detail
  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Hand skills and coordination

Would you enjoy a career that combines science, teamwork, decision-making, and hands-on work?
