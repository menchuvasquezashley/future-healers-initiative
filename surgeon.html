Surgeon

Surgeons are doctors who perform operations to treat certain injuries, diseases, and other medical conditions.

🏥 Where do they work? Hospitals, surgical centers, and clinics.
👥 Who do they work with? Nurses, anesthesiologists, surgical technologists, and other healthcare professionals.

🩺 What do they do?

Surgeons meet with patients, help plan treatment, perform procedures, and follow up with patients afterward.

There are also many different types of surgeons, so their work can look very different depending on their specialty.

📚 Education Path

  1. High school
  2. College
  3. Medical school
  4. Residency and surgical training

Becoming a surgeon takes many years of education and training.

🧠 Skills that can help

  • Problem-solving
  • Teamwork
  • Communication
  • Focus and attention to detail
  • Being comfortable with hands-on work

💭 Think About It

Would you enjoy solving problems, working with a team, and doing hands-on work?

If so, surgery could be a career worth exploring more.
